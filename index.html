About Us

Where flavor meets atmosphere

Aquanetta Amour is a foody brand built around one idea: food and drink should feel like an occasion. We're the team behind Foody — a running collection of cocktail recipes, seasonal dishes, and behind-the-scenes stories from a kitchen that takes its ambience as seriously as its ingredients.

From pomegranate-bright cocktails to slow-simmered comfort food, every recipe we share is tested, tasted, and styled the way we'd actually serve it — dim lighting, good glassware, and all.
